# Plan 04-02 — Ledger write-side atomicity: save_portfolio + save_journal to tmp+os.replace (D-04..D-06)

## User-story framing

**As a** consumer reading my 持仓/账本 over the token-gated endpoints (Phase 4 STA-02), **I want to** know the ledger files behind those endpoints can never be observed half-written, even in the microsecond window a save is happening — **so that** no tool of mine ever serves me a truncated position list as if it were real, and the Phase 2 read-side defense gets a write side that matches it.

<objective>
Rework exactly two writer functions in `scripts/daily/trading_journal.py` — `save_portfolio` (L45-47) and `save_journal` (L57-59) — from their current direct `open('w')` truncating writes to the D-05-prescribed tmp + `os.replace` atomic pattern (zt_pool.py:72-78 template), and pin the atomicity contract in the new `tests/test_trading_journal.py` suite (no .tmp residue on success, target keeps previous good content on a forced replace failure, unchanged round-trip through `load_portfolio`/`load_journal`, module-global monkeypatch seam, zero contact with real logs/).

Purpose: D-04..D-06 (user-signed in Phase 2/3 discuss, deferred to Phase 4) bind write-side atomicity as the mandatory prerequisite before the STA-02 portfolio/journal reads go live — this Wave-1 plan lands it before 04-03 builds the private read surface on top, and before 04-07's real-machine gate can ever serve a torn ledger over HTTP. Scope stays at the two functions per D-05 (no logs/ sweep); `record_hold_valuation`'s `daily_valuations.json` write and all other script paths stay untouched.

Output: `scripts/daily/trading_journal.py` (two atomic writers, byte-compatible output), `tests/test_trading_journal.py`, green suite.
</objective>
